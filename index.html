01 / The idea

Data is useful.
Meaning is powerful.

Most sustainability infrastructure produces numbers. CommunityHub builds the layer that helps people relate those numbers to the places they inhabit.

We acquire and organize real-time resource data, then translate it into dashboards, digital signage, embeddable visualizations and community stories.

01 — ENGAGE↗

Bring people in

Digital signage, phone experiences, calendars and community voices make sustainability part of everyday life.

02 — EDUCATE↗

Make systems legible

Real-time electricity, water, heat and environmental conditions become visual, navigable stories rather than spreadsheets.

03 — EMPOWER↗

Turn insight into action

Show people how their choices connect to buildings, campuses and the larger community — and make positive action visible.
