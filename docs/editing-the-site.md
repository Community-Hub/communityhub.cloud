# Changing the words on communityhub.cloud yourself

For John and Maddie. The site has its own editor, so you can change wording, links, pictures and lists without asking Kwaku. Nothing you do there can break the layout: the editor only accepts plain text.

## Signing in

There is no password.

1. Open **https://editor.communityhb.tech** (the editor lives on its own address; the public site has no edit button).
2. Type your email address (the one Kwaku added for you) and press **Email me a code**.
3. A 6-digit code arrives in your inbox within a minute (check junk if it does not). Type it in and press **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on that browser for a week. After that, or on another computer, ask for a new code the same way.

## Change a sentence

After you sign in you are on the site itself, with a dark bar at the bottom of the screen.

1. Go to the page you want. To move between pages, press **Browse** in the bar, use the site's menu as usual, then press **Edit text** again.
2. Move the pointer over the page: anything you can change lights up. Click the sentence.
3. A small box opens with the wording. Change it and press **Save**. The page shows your new wording at once, so you can see how it fits.
4. On the public site it shows after about a minute: refresh the page, and if it still shows the old wording, refresh once more.

To go back, click the sentence again and press **Use the original wording**.

### Bold and italic

The box has a **B** button and an *I* button above the wording.

- To change a few words, select them in the box, then press **B** for bold or **I** for italic.
- To change the whole wording, press **B** or **I** without selecting anything.
- Press the same button again to take it off. The box shows the wording as it will look.
- Press **Save**.

If your text runs much longer than the original, the box warns you, because long text may not fit its space on the page.

A few pieces of wording cannot be clicked on the page (text inside the live dashboards, and a few lines the page reshapes). For those, and for pictures and lists, press **Pictures, lists and history** in the bar: it opens the full list, where **Words and links** has a search box (**Find a word or heading**).

## Change a picture

1. Press **Pictures, lists and history** in the bar, open **Pictures** and find the picture.
2. Upload a jpg, png or webp (up to 4 MB). A **picture description** is required: one sentence saying what the picture shows, for people who can't see it.
3. Press **Save**.

## Lists, and hiding a section

- **Lists** (lessons, publications, quotes, pricing bundles, FAQ and so on): add, remove or reorder items, then press **Save list**.
- **Show or hide**: hide or show whole sections such as Supporters or Questions.

## Undo anything

**History** lists every change: who made it, when, and the old and new wording. Press **Put back** on any entry, or **Reset to original** on any sentence, list or picture, to return to the wording or picture that shipped with the site.

## Good to know

- An item marked **Out of date** means the site's built-in wording changed after you edited it, so the original is showing. Open it and press Save again to keep your version.
- Headings and words inside the live dashboards, calendars and stories come from those products, not from this editor.
